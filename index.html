How to type answers
  • Powers: x^3, (2x+1)^4, e^(3x). Use parentheses around multi-term exponents.
  • Functions: sin(x), cos x, sec^2(x), ln(x), sqrt(x), arctan(x), |x|.
  • Constants: pi, e. Fractions like 3/2 are fine.
  • Multiplication can be implicit: 2x sin(x) or x(x+1).
  • Antiderivatives: include + C. Any equivalent form is accepted.
  • Enter to check, Enter again for the next problem.
